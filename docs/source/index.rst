OpenSTEF Documentation
======================

OpenSTEF is a Python machine learning library for short-term energy forecasting in the electricity sector. It provides a complete pipeline for training models, creating forecasts, and evaluating results across various energy applications including grid congestion management, transport forecasting, and renewable energy prediction.

Community
---------

OpenSTEF is developed as an open source project under the LF Energy Foundation. Join our community through the `GitHub repository <https://github.com/OpenSTEF/openstef>`_ for code contributions and issue tracking, or connect with other users in the `LF Energy Slack workspace <https://slack.lfenergy.org/>`_ (#openstef channel). We hold community meetings every four weeks - find meeting details on our `wiki page <https://wiki.lfenergy.org/display/OS/Four-weekly+community+meeting>`_.
